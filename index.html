眼见为实
Show, don't tell

删了，空间没变？
因为还没真正释放。

Deleted, but storage unchanged?
Because it's not reclaimed yet.

iPhone 的删除是两段式的：照片进入「最近删除」后，30 天内仍然占着空间。所以很多人兴冲冲删了几千张，一看存储数字纹丝不动，就再也不试了。

Deleting on iPhone is two-stage: photos stay in "Recently Deleted" and keep occupying space for 30 days. That's why people delete thousands, see no change in storage, and never try again.

简相册不把这两件事混为一谈。它分别告诉你移走了多少、真正释放了多少、清空之后预计还剩多少。清空完再回来，它还会重新核对一遍。

Simple Album doesn't blur the two. It tells you what moved, what was actually reclaimed, and what to expect after emptying. Come back afterwards and it re-checks.
