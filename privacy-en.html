Simple Album · Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · applies to version 0.31 and later

In one line: Simple Album collects nothing and does not use the network.

Your photos stay on your iPhone. We do not read them, transmit them, store them elsewhere, or share them.

1. What we read

To find duplicate photos, similar photos, screenshots, screen recordings and large files, Simple Album reads your photo library, limited to:

  • Image content, used for feature extraction and comparison — computed on the device
  • Basic metadata such as capture date, resolution and file size
  • System flags that mark screenshots and screen recordings (we do not guess from pixels)

All of this is processed in memory and in a local database on your device. It never leaves the device.

2. What we do not do

  • No networking. The app contains no network request code, no server, no cloud analysis.
  • No uploads. Photos, videos and analysis results are never sent anywhere.
  • No accounts. No sign-up, no sign-in, no phone number or email collected.
  • No tracking. No advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking.
  • No third-party SDKs. No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting components.
  • No advertising. There are no ads in the app.

3. Purchases

Unlocking similar photos and videos is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. No subscription, no auto-renewal.

The purchase is handled by Apple's App Store. We never see or store your payment method, card or billing address — Apple processes that under its own privacy policy. The app only learns whether a purchase already exists.

4. Where data is stored

  • Fingerprints and grouping results, so the next launch does not need a full rescan — kept in a local database inside the app's sandbox.
  • Your preferences (free allowance usage, confirm-before-delete, and similar), kept in system preferences.

Everything sits inside the app sandbox, unreadable by other apps. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

5. Photo library permission

You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Simple Album. Without it the app cannot read your library; nothing already computed is uploaded, because it never left the device.

6. Deletion happens in two stages

Deletion moves photos into the system's Recently Deleted album, where they stay for 30 days and still occupy space. You can restore them during that period. Simple Album does not bypass this and never empties Recently Deleted for you — that final step is yours to tap.

7. Children

Simple Album is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone — we collect none at all.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated and the app will note it. Anything affecting data collection would be spelled out in the release notes.
