FAQ

1. The home screen shows all zeros

Look for an orange banner at the top. If it is there, photo access is set to "Limited" — iOS only lets Simple Album see the photos you hand-picked. Tap "Allow more photos" and choose "Allow Full Access", or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Simple Album. The app refreshes by itself when you come back.

2. The first scan is taking a long time

That is expected. Analyzing tens of thousands of photos happens entirely on this phone, and the screen keeps showing progress and how many items have been processed. Later launches only do an incremental comparison.

3. It says "Your library looks clean"

That message only appears when there is genuinely nothing to clean within the visible library. It is suppressed under limited photo access, where the app can only see part of your library.

4. How does the free allowance work

For similar photos and videos: the first 50 groups are free, counted per group, cumulative for the lifetime of the install (it does not reset after you delete). Duplicate photos, duplicate videos, screenshots, screen recordings, large files and video compression are free forever, with no limit. After the one-time purchase there are no limits at all.

5. I bought it before — do I pay again on a new phone?

No. Tap Settings → Restore purchase, or the "Restore purchase" link at the bottom of the paywall. It works for the same Apple ID and for Family Sharing members.

6. Can deleted photos be recovered?

Yes. Simple Album uses the standard system deletion, so photos move to "Recently Deleted" and stay there for 30 days. You can restore them any time in that window. Simple Album never empties Recently Deleted for you.

7. I deleted thousands of photos but storage did not change

Because photos in "Recently Deleted" still occupy space. To actually reclaim it, empty Recently Deleted in the Photos app. The completion screen separates three numbers: moved to Recently Deleted, actually reclaimed, and expected free space after emptying.

8. Are my photos uploaded?

No. Simple Album contains no networking code; all analysis happens on device. See the Privacy Policy.

9. Requirements

iPhone, iOS 16 or later. Portrait only.

10. The suggested keep is wrong — can I change it?

Yes. You can pick which photo to keep in every group, or select several. By default, groups that need your judgment start unchecked: only what you have actually looked at gets deleted.
